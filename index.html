About

Zain operates at an unusual intersection — civic institution-building, social enterprise, and applied commercial work. Each venture has been deliberate, not opportunistic.

He founded Empowerment Pathways Network at 20, leading it through its complete lifecycle — design, growth, and a responsible wind-down in 2025. Not every founder knows when to close with integrity.

Today he co-directs Safak Pakistan, a non-partisan think tank building analytical capacity among Pakistan's next generation, while grounding his work in real-world infrastructure project analysis at Al-Meezan Engineering.

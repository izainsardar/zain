IProfileLahore, Pakistan

Deliberate, not opportunistic.

Muhammad Zain works where commercial enterprise, public infrastructure and civic institution-building meet. Each undertaking has been chosen with intent, and each is judged by what it leaves in place.

He is Founder and Chief Executive of Mukhtar, a corporate concierge that takes on the operational requirements of businesses through a vetted professional network. At Al-Meezan Engineering & Management Services he serves as Director of Project Governance and Digitalization, responsible for the oversight and digital systems behind the firm's government-contracted infrastructure work.

He founded his first organization, Empowerment Pathways Network, at twenty and led it through its full course: design, growth and a responsible close in 2025. Knowing when to conclude a venture with integrity is, in his view, part of leading one. He is also Secretary-General and Co-Founder of Safak Pakistan, a non-partisan think tank building analytical capacity among the country's next generation.
